## B. Full version

### 1. Neto is an information tool

Neto shows the data you enter or import and calculations based on it. Neto does not provide investment advice or any other investment services within the meaning of the Polish Act of 29 July 2005 on Trading in Financial Instruments, does not prepare investment recommendations, does not give tax or legal advice, does not accept money and does not place orders.

Nothing in Neto — net worth, result, share of your wealth, goal pace, retirement scenario, the "On track" or "Behind pace" label — is an indication that you should buy, sell or hold any instrument or change the way you save. Neto does not assess your financial situation or compare you with others.

### 2. Market data may be delayed or wrong

- Exchange rates come from National Bank of Poland (NBP) tables (the average rate from the last business day). On weekends and public holidays we use the rate from the previous business day.
- Instrument prices come from [PRICING PROVIDER] or from you (a manual price). Valuation runs once a day in the evening; prices are not updated live during the day.
- Every valuation shows the date of its price. A price older than 3 business days and an asset valuation not updated for 90 days are marked.
- A data source may make a mistake or be temporarily unavailable. We do not guarantee that market data is correct, complete or up to date.

### 3. Calculations are estimates

- **Deposits**: value with accrued interest at the rate and compounding you entered, before tax.
- **Retail treasury bonds**: value by the formula for the bond type and the interest rates you enter for each period; without the early redemption fee and without tax.
- **Cost basis and result**: FIFO method; the result in PLN is split into the instrument's result and the currency effect. Neto does not calculate tax, and the result may differ from your broker's statement or your tax return.
- **Rate of return (XIRR)**: depends on how completely you recorded deposits and withdrawals; when it cannot be calculated we show "—".
- **Ownership share**: PLN values are multiplied by the share you set; this is not a legal determination of co-ownership.

### 4. The retirement calculator is a simulation

The calculator works in today's złoty, based on assumptions you can see and change (including the real rate of return in three scenarios, the withdrawal rate, contributions, expenses and the expected state pension from ZUS). Actual returns, inflation, taxes and pension rules may differ from the assumptions. Past results do not guarantee future results. The calculator's output is not a forecast or a promise.

### 5. Investment risk

The value of financial instruments, cryptocurrencies, property and currencies can go down as well as up; you may lose part or all of the money invested. Exchange rate movements affect the value of assets in foreign currencies. Neto shows these changes but does not protect you from them.

### 6. Your decisions

You make financial decisions yourself. If you need advice tailored to your situation, use a licensed investment adviser, tax adviser or other qualified professional.

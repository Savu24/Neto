You can delete your account yourself, on the web or in the iPhone app. You don't need to contact us.

## Before you delete

If you want to keep your data, download it first: **Settings → Data → Export**. You'll get a ZIP file with your data in JSON and CSV.

## On the web

1. Sign in to Neto.
2. Open **Settings → Data**.
3. Choose **Delete account**.
4. Confirm with your password or a code from your 2FA app.

## On iPhone

1. Open the Neto app and sign in.
2. Tap **More → Settings → Data**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm with your password or a code from your 2FA app.

Deleting the app from your phone does **not** delete your account.

## What happens next

- You are signed out on all devices right away, and a confirmation is sent to your account's e-mail address.
- For **14 days** the account waits for deletion. If you change your mind, just sign in during that time — the deletion will be cancelled.
- After 14 days we permanently delete the account and all data: accounts, transactions, valuations, goals, settings, notes, sessions and notification tokens. This cannot be undone.
- Data disappears from backups no later than after [BACKUP PERIOD].
- We keep only [RETAINED DATA] so that we can show the deletion was carried out.

If you sign in with Google, you can also remove Neto's access in your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party apps & services).

## Can't access your account?

Write to [PRIVACY E-MAIL] from the e-mail address linked to the account and ask for deletion. If you write from another address, we'll ask you to confirm the account is yours. We reply within one month.
